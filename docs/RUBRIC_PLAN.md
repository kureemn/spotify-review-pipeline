## Predeclared golden-set evaluation protocol

1. **Isolation.** Golden texts are never used in prompts, examples, thresholds or issue discovery. The human labels them blind: the page shows the text only, without the star rating. Their labels never reach any model.
2. **Predictions.** The scored predictions are the golden IDs' records from the **final full run**. The final run's config is frozen first, and the golden results are computed afterwards.
3. **Metrics (strict and accepted-set).** Topic and intent agreement, macro F1 over unambiguous singleton cases, severity exact agreement and MAE, and a joint (all three correct) rate. The *accepted-set* variant also counts labels the human listed as acceptable for ambiguous cases, mirroring the instructor checker's `accepted_*` lists. Missing or quarantined predictions count as **incorrect**.
4. **Sentiment.** Mean absolute error, plus agreement within a predeclared tolerance of **±0.5** on the −1..1 scale.
5. **Evidence quote.** Exact-substring membership is checked by code. Whether the quote supports the label is judged per case by a human: supports / partial / does not.
6. **Entities.** Count unsupported additions, meaning entities not in the human list and not in the text. Lexicon extraction can never add a term the text lacks, so the analysis looks at missed and over-broad matches.
7. **needs_review.** Evaluated as a prediction of the human "ambiguous or needs review" flag, with precision and recall reported; it is not treated as correctness.
8. **Ambiguity.** Report the number of ambiguous cases. Hard cases are never dropped.
9. **No tuning on golden.** Prompt changes use development data only: the 500/10k checkpoints and verifier disagreements. If golden results ever influence a revision, we disclose it and add **fresh held-out cases** (labeled from unused full-corpus IDs) for the final check.
10. Fifty cases are a diagnostic sample, not a population accuracy estimate.

## Verification protocol (predeclared)

- **Main run:** sample = `sha256("verify-v1:" + review_id) / 16^12 < 0.02` over model-labelled records, about 9.7k reviews. **Pilot:** rate 0.20.
- The verifier receives the original text and rubric only. Code compares topic, intent and severity, and reports agreement, exact severity, severity within ±1, MAE and a confusion table.
- Disagreements are inspected and summarized with concrete cases.

## Cost & control targets

- Professor's cap: **≤ $10 total**, enforced in code across all runs (`project_budget_usd`). Plan: pilot 100 → 500 → 10k → full, refreshing the estimate at each step.
- Enrichment and verification use the `flex` tier (50% of standard, per the model page). The tier actually used is recorded per call, and the calculator prices each call at that tier.
