Notes

Rules that decide most cases

  • Label the text. Star rating is hidden on purpose.
  • Topic = highest-severity specific problem; tie → first mentioned. Positive review → first specific praised feature; general praise → other.
  • Mentioning Premium alone ≠ billing. Premium-only controls (skip/repeat/choose songs) = billing. Crash for a paying user = playback.
  • Mixed praise + criticism = complaint. Generic "bad app" = complaint, topic other.
  • Bare boycott slogans / unrelated text = unclear.
  • Cancellation, stars or angry words do not raise severity.
