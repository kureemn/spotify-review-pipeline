**Reduce free-tier friction while protecting the listening experience**

## Recommendation
Prioritize **billing/support**, focused first on clarifying and reviewing free-tier restrictions, then reducing ad interruptions and making the value exchange easier to understand. The largest actionable specific issue is the perceived removal of basic listening controls behind Premium: it generated 46 complaints [C001] and the highest priority score among the listed issues, 134 [C004]. Treat this as a product-policy and communication problem, not simply a support-volume problem. Validate which restrictions are intentional, explain them in context, and assess whether selected controls can be restored or offered more transparently.

A reviewer describes several ads in succession (review b9ae23d2-1152-4bfe-a876-afc09c005377). 3. **Playback reliability as a safeguard.** Playback reports are fewer in the leading specific issues, but can prevent the core service from working. Stops or failures account for 12 complaints [C017], and connectivity errors for 8 [C021]. A reviewer says a playlist would not play (review 06d4c78d-73b4-4b54-9ef3-3d5a0d18cb96). Keep reliability monitoring in scope while addressing monetization friction.

## Limitations
This is self-selected public Google Play review evidence from 2022-05-19 to 2023-11-15, not a representative user sample or a measure of revenue, retention, or confirmed churn. The source has 500 records; all 500 nonempty reviews were classified, with 0 unresolved or quarantined nonempty reviews. Exact-text reuse affected 21 records, which may reduce independence. Cancellation language is intent, not observed cancellation. The issue ranking includes broad general dissatisfaction (52 complaints [C005]) that is coherent but not diagnostically actionable. Some classifications are mixed, especially redesign and playback issues; labels and inferred severity should be validated before committing to a specific fix.
